# 09. Animation and audio

## Time on screen (proposed)

The world advances in whole hours; the viewer makes them flow:

- **One in-game hour** plays in about one second at normal speed; speeds
  x0.25 to x8, and step by hour.
- **Movement** tweened between tiles over the hour; an action plays a short
  loop (chopping, sowing, building) on the persona's tile.
- **Refusals** show: an action the rules refused flashes and its reason
  appears in the panel, since a refusal is often the story.
- **Speech** appears as a bubble for a few seconds, longer lines truncated.

## Day, night, seasons (proposed)

- A light cycle over the 24 hours: dawn and dusk tints, night darker with
  fires and houses lit.
- Seasons as palette shifts of the same tiles: green spring, gold summer,
  rust autumn, pale winter with snow on the ground.
- Weather as a light overlay (rain, snow), drawn procedurally like
  avatar7's ambient layers.

## Events worth a beat (proposed)

- Goal met: a short flourish over the map.
- A persona faints or leaves: its sprite fades, its last line stays.
- A broken promise: a mark between the two personas, visible in the
  Together bar.

## Audio (optional, proposed)

- **Ambience** per season and time of day; a few loops, quiet.
- **Music**: one theme per season, made with flux7-audio, or none; the
  replay is often watched while reading.
- **Voices**: optional, the personas' lines spoken by a local voice
  (Piper), the way avatar7 speaks; off by default.

## Open

- How much animation the first viewer needs: a static map stepping by hour
  is enough to judge whether personas are interesting; motion comes after.
